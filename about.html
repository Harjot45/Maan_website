Who we are

Freight is more than point A to B.

Kuninda Group is an Indigenous-owned Australian transport and logistics business supporting freight movements across the country. We work with businesses that need a clear, practical approach to moving machinery, steel, general freight, refrigerated goods and bulk loads.

Every job begins with the details: what is being moved, where it needs to go, when it is needed, and what will happen at each site. Those details shape the transport discussion and help us identify a suitable way forward.

Whether you are planning a one-off movement, need support with a recurring freight requirement, or want to discuss a subcontracting opportunity, you can speak directly with the team.

How we work

Attention to the details that matter.

Transport plans work best when the right information is shared early.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the freight

We ask about the item, approximate weight and dimensions, packaging, handling needs and any temperature or restraint requirements.

02 / PLAN

Discuss the route

Pickup and delivery locations, access conditions, deadlines and unloading arrangements all influence the transport approach.

03 / CONNECT

Keep contact clear

Direct communication helps you share changes, ask questions and stay aligned on what the job requires.
